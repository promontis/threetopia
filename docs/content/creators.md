# Threetopia Creators

[creators.threetopia.com](https://creators.threetopia.com) is the home for your account and packages.

## My packages

See your asset and world packages, newest version, state and installation count. Open a package to inspect every version, its changelog, server validation report and preview. Only your account can see your unpublished work.

A ready version can be published directly from its card. For a world, **Publish to this tile** releases its map content on the bound tile. The tile cannot be swapped underneath an existing package.

## Tiles: My tiles and Explore

**My tiles** lists your reserved and published tiles. Open the linked world package, create one for an unused reservation, or use **View on map** to inspect that exact tile within **My tiles**.

Tile cards load saved photographs of the actual 3D map. Threetopia automatically creates a new image after a reservation, a host change, a validated world-package upload, or publication. Nearby tile changes also refresh images when they affect the view. The previous photograph stays visible while an update is prepared; opening **My tiles** does not render the 3D map.

Your own photograph includes your latest validated map content, even before publication. Other creators only see published content. An incomplete or rejected upload does not replace the current photograph. **View on map** opens the interactive view.

**Explore** opens the actual Three.js map, including the existing Lagoon, Tidewater, Sakura and Punk showcase worlds. Every free position sharing an edge with a published world is available, including positions beyond an unfinished inner ring. Only free positions can be selected here. Published worlds and reservations remain visible for context; they do not open detail cards or change the selected position. Click a free position directly on the map. New neighboring positions appear when a world is published.

Free positions appear as empty, raised tiles. Selecting one zooms the map to that tile and opens its options beside it (below it on narrow screens). Close the panel or press **Escape** to return to the overview.

The tile options let you choose among fifteen authored host designs, with nature, waterfront, city and floating Punk environments. The selected tile and its 3D preview show the exact procedural host geometry. Reserve the position and variant, then create your world package. The host terrain and slot dimensions are fixed from that point onward.

## Packages: Explore

Browse published packages, inspect their versions and changelogs, and copy the install command. Prefer reusable components over rebuilding them independently. Use the CLI to install exact versions into your project.

## Installed by creators

Your package page lists creators with active CLI installations, the version they use and the number of installations. These records update when a creator installs, updates or uninstalls. They are not browser analytics and do not expose private source code, project names or email addresses.

## Account

Open your account from the avatar in the sidebar. Edit your profile, sign out, install the CLI, or revoke terminal access there. Your browser session and each CLI token are separate credentials.

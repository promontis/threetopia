A few practical questions

Can I bring a project I already maintain?

Yes. Your project can stay in its own repo. A standalone demo may need an adapter: it must use the world’s renderer and controls, expose update and cleanup hooks, and declare the assets it uses.

Do I need to build a whole scene?

No. Grass, water, materials, birds or a single building can all be contributions. Other creators can use those parts in their scenes.

Do our scenes need to look alike?

The interiors can have their own style. Shared border geometry, material transitions and lighting rules help the joins feel continuous. Exactly how much freedom that leaves is something we want to test with the first creators.

What if two tiles don’t match?

Rotate the tile, choose a compatible neighbour or build a transition tile. All six boundaries need to fit, including their corner heights. Matching declarations still need a preview together; they do not prove that the actual meshes line up.

Can I explore Threetopia yet?

Yes. Walk from Lagoon Tree Village through Sakura River Valley and Tidewater to Threejs-Punk in one connected world. Step inside the world. You’ll need a browser with WebGPU. The creator tools are still in development.

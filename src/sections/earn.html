04 · Creators earn

Your €8 follows your choices.

The world is free to explore. Builders subscribe for €10 a month: €2 funds Threetopia, €8 is your creator allocation. It's split evenly over the root components you explicitly chose, then shared within each root by everyone whose verified work it builds on. Not downloads, not views, not likes: verified use.

Weighted by depth, not by package count

Inside a root, each creator receives weight 1 / 2depth: the root author 1, direct dependencies ½, the next level ¼. Packages from one payout account collapse into one share at their shallowest depth.

  • Downloads don't pay. Nor do views, stars, CI reinstalls or 100,000 copies of one mesh.
  • Splitting doesn't pay. Five packages by Alex still count as one Alex.
  • You never pay yourself. Self-only roots can't dilute the creators you depend on.
  • It always reconciles. Integer cents, deterministic rounding, reversals instead of edits.

Prototype policy. The €10 price and 20/80 split are assumptions to be validated; payments in the prototype are simulated.
